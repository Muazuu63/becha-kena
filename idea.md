Business Idea Proposition
1. Business Name
বেচা-কেনা (Becha-Kena)

2. Project Overview
Becha-Kena is a C2C (Customer-to-Customer) online marketplace designed to buy and sell second-hand products in Bangladesh safely. Inspired by the core classified listing model of Bikroy.com, Becha-Kena fixes the biggest problem in the second-hand market: Trust and Fraud.

By introducing mandatory NID (National ID) Verification for both buyers and sellers, Becha-Kena creates a verified, safe, and accountable community where users can deal with real people without the fear of fake listings, stolen products, or scams.

3. The Core Problem We Solve
Anonymity & Scams: Unverified users posting fake ads, selling stolen items, or disappearing after taking advance payments.

Lack of Trust: Buyers hesitate to deal directly with strangers due to zero identity tracking or safety guarantees.

4. Our Key Solution (The USP)
Mandatory NID Verification: Every buyer and seller must verify their profile using their Bangladeshi NID card before posting an ad or sending messages.

Verified Profiles: Every user displays a "Verified User" status, creating full accountability and driving fraud down to near zero.

Simple Classified Model: Keeps the smooth, familiar, and easy listing experience of Bikroy.com (browse, chat, meet up, and deal).

5. Value Proposition
For Sellers:

Sell pre-owned items faster to genuine, verified buyers.

Avoid fake inquiries and non-serious spammers.

For Buyers:

Shop pre-owned products with complete peace of mind.

Deal directly with real individuals whose real-world identity is verified.

6. How It Works
Sign Up & Verify: Users register and complete instant NID verification.

Post or Search: Verified sellers list their products; verified buyers search for deals nearby.

Connect & Deal: Buyers and sellers chat directly on the platform, meet up safely, and complete the transaction.
